# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name

Pavementa

## Tagline

Road Intelligence for Modern Cities

## Tone

Pavementa should communicate in a professional, civic-tech, trustworthy, and modern voice. The product should feel credible to councils, contractors, public-sector teams, infrastructure managers, and technical reviewers.

The tone should be:

- Clear
- Operational
- Data-led
- Confident
- Practical
- Public-sector appropriate
- Modern without feeling trendy

## Colour Direction

Use a restrained, professional infrastructure palette:

- Dark navy for authority, structure, and enterprise polish
- White for clarity and clean workspace surfaces
- Slate grey for interface depth, text, borders, and neutral states
- Infrastructure green for status, progress, and resolved repair signals
- Safety amber for warnings, severity indicators, and attention states

## Avoid

- Cartoon potholes
- Student-style UI
- Cheap clipart
- Overly playful illustrations
- Decorative visuals that undermine product credibility
- Overcrowded dashboards without clear operational hierarchy
- Generic smart-city cliches without practical workflow value
