# Project Overview

Pavementa is a civic-tech road infrastructure intelligence platform built for organisations responsible for maintaining public road networks. The platform is intended for councils, contractors, local authorities, inspection teams, and infrastructure decision-makers who need reliable evidence, clear prioritisation, and professional reporting.

The product combines road image collection, AI-assisted surface damage detection, structured damage records, map-based visualisation, and reporting workflows. Its purpose is to help infrastructure teams move from fragmented inspection notes and manual spreadsheets toward a central operational system for identifying, tracking, and acting on road defects.

Pavementa should feel like a serious SaaS product: dependable, secure, data-led, and suitable for public-sector and contractor environments. The interface should prioritise clarity, trust, repeatable workflows, and evidence-based decision-making over decorative visuals.

The long-term vision is to support the full damage intelligence lifecycle:

- Capture road condition evidence from field images
- Detect potholes, cracks, and other road surface issues
- Assign severity and location metadata
- Store inspection records in a searchable registry
- Visualise network condition on a GIS map
- Generate professional reports for maintenance planning
- Track cases from detection through repair completion
- Provide analytics for budget planning and long-term infrastructure trends
